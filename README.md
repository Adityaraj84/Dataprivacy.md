## Check what information IRCTC collects

According to IRCTC's privacy policy, registration information can include:

Name
Gender
Marital status
Age/Date of birth
Nationality
Email address
Postal address
Contact number

IRCTC also states that it may collect transactional history and information about other travellers for whom a booking is made.
